## 先说明你要找什么样的鞋

寻找女鞋供应商时，“我想要凉鞋”还不够具体。人字凉鞋、多带拖鞋、后带凉鞋和厚底款，在结构与风格上都有区别。

与供应商联系前，可以先准备参考图片或款号、目标市场、尺码范围、计划数量，以及希望调整的颜色或细节。不确定的地方也可以直接说明，作为双方讨论的起点。

你可以先浏览我们的[近期产品图册](/new-arrivals/)，再带着感兴趣的型号与我们沟通。例如，[9339-19 多带拖鞋](/new-arrivals/9339-19/)与[8228-5 后带凉鞋](/new-arrivals/8228-5/)代表不同的产品方向。

## 图片之外，确认样品的具体细节

图片帮助判断外观，样品则让双方能够围绕同一件实物讨论。建议在确认前列清楚：

- 鞋面与鞋底材料，以及颜色和表面效果。
- 尺码标注、对应尺寸和试穿反馈。
- 装饰、接缝、边缘与其他需要关注的细节。
- Logo、包装以及需要修改的位置。

对脚的理解也是我们持续记录的话题。好看的轮廓值得欣赏，实际的穿着感受同样值得认真讨论。具体感受需要结合样品和使用者反馈，不能只凭照片判断。

## 区分选款、贴牌与定制需求

如果你在寻找 OEM/ODM 鞋厂，建议不要只问“能不能定制”，而是把需求拆开：是选择已有款式、调整颜色、增加品牌标识，还是根据参考开发新的结构？

不同项目的可行性和安排需要分别确认。联系 WOOOHU 时，可以直接发来参考与需求，我们再沟通适合的合作方式。

报价需要对应具体条件。询价时，可以一起发送型号、预计数量、尺码与配色分配、品牌或包装要求、目的地，以及期望的时间安排。

## How to Choose a Women’s Sandals and Slides Supplier

Finding a new footwear supplier often begins with a product image that catches your eye. Building a working relationship takes a little more: clear expectations, physical samples and careful communication.

We are WOOOHU (Gaomeilun), a women’s sandals and fashion slides manufacturer based in Jieyang, Guangdong, China. Here are the questions we suggest discussing when you begin sourcing a new collection.

### 1. Define the footwear you want to source

“Women’s sandals” is a starting point, rather than a complete brief. Thong sandals, multi-strap slides, slingback sandals and platform styles involve different silhouettes and construction details.

Prepare reference images or style numbers, your target market, size range, estimated quantity and the colours or details you would like to explore. If some decisions are still open, say so: they can become part of the discussion.

Browse our [latest footwear collections](/new-arrivals/) to identify a direction. For example, [style 9339-19 multi-strap slides](/new-arrivals/9339-19/) and [style 8228-5 slingback sandals](/new-arrivals/8228-5/) offer different starting points for a sourcing conversation.

### 2. Look beyond the photograph

Images communicate appearance. A physical sample gives both sides something specific to examine and discuss. Before approving a sample, clarify:

- Upper and sole materials, colours and surface finishes.
- Size markings, corresponding measurements and fit feedback.
- Embellishments, seams, edges and other details important to your project.
- Logo placement, packaging and any requested changes.

Understanding feet is an ongoing interest behind our [Notes on Feet](/blog/). Appearance matters, and so does the experience of wearing a shoe. Fit and feel should be discussed through samples and wearer feedback, rather than assumed from a photograph.

### 3. Make OEM and ODM enquiries specific

If you are looking for an OEM/ODM footwear manufacturer, explain what customisation means for your project. Are you selecting an existing style, changing colours, adding your brand identity, or exploring a different construction from a reference?

Feasibility and arrangements need to be confirmed for each project. Share your references with WOOOHU so we can discuss a suitable way forward.

### 4. Give your supplier enough information to quote

For a useful wholesale enquiry, include the style number, estimated order quantity, size and colour breakdown, branding or packaging requirements, destination and intended schedule.

Minimum order quantities, sample costs, production lead times and shipping arrangements depend on the style and project. These should be confirmed directly; there is no single promise that fits every order.
