脚对于人，非常重要。

只是我们往往习惯了它的支撑，直到经历长时间的行走、持续的站立，或某一次艰难的考验，才开始认真感受它的存在。

在考验到来之前，我们是否想过：双脚需要怎样的照顾，又需要怎样的保护？

我们想从这些问题出发，持续观察、思考和研究双脚，也重新理解鞋与脚的关系。

在往后的日子里，我们会在这里分享对脚的认知、做鞋过程中的发现，以及仍在探索的问题。

希望这些分享能帮助你更好地了解和照顾自己的双脚，让每一步走得更从容，让生活也因此变得更好。

---

## English

Our feet matter.

Yet we often take their support for granted. Only after a long walk, hours of standing, or a demanding experience do we begin to notice how much we ask of them.

Before those challenges arrive, do we pause to consider the care and protection our feet need?

These questions are our starting point. We want to keep observing, learning, and thinking about feet—and how shoes can better serve them.

In the days ahead, we will share what we learn, what we discover through making shoes, and the questions we are still exploring.

We hope these notes help you understand and care for your feet, so you can move through life with greater comfort and confidence.
