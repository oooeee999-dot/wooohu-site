Privacy & analytics

Updated: October 28, 2026

With your permission, WOOOHU uses Google Analytics to understand website visits, viewed pages, approximate visitor regions, referral sources, and clicks on WhatsApp or email links. Contact-button clicks do not tell us whether a message was sent or a sale occurred.

Optional analytics starts only after you accept. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes technical and usage information. We do not deliberately send your messages, email address or phone number as analytics event parameters. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our configuration.

You can reject analytics or change your choice using Privacy settings in the footer. Your choice is saved in this browser. Blocking or rejecting analytics means our reports will not count every visitor.

Learn more about Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner sites. If you contact us through email or WhatsApp, your chosen service handles that communication.
